## Worth having, even if you're not sure

This is a short window in your life and a piece of your child's history that
nobody will remember except through pictures. The goal isn't a perfect
photograph. It's something you're glad later that you have.

A lot of people book this while feeling slightly self-conscious about it. That's
common, and it doesn't show up in the pictures.

## Timing

Somewhere around 28 to 34 weeks is a common sweet spot, but there isn't a single
right answer. Earlier usually means moving more easily and a smaller bump. Later
gives the fuller, more traditional maternity shape.

How you're feeling and how your pregnancy is actually going matter more than the
number of weeks. This is a general guide, not medical advice.

## You decide who's in it

Just you. You and your partner. Your older children. Your dog. A mix — plenty of
people do some of just her first, then bring everyone in.

If you have older kids, we'll usually photograph you first and add them
afterward, so the images of you are finished before anyone's patience runs out.

## You decide what it looks like

Flowing dress, ordinary clothes, bare belly, covered belly, traditional, natural,
somewhere in between. You supply your own clothing. Solids tend to photograph
particularly well because they keep attention where you want it.

Bring what means something — baby shoes, an ultrasound image, a rattle, a
blanket with the name on it if you know it and you're announcing it. I have an
announcement message board you're welcome to use.

## Where

Outdoors, at the same locations as any other session. Sometimes a nursery or a
room at home works beautifully if the light is right — ask and we'll figure it
out.

## If you don't know what you want yet

Scroll the Pinterest boards and pay attention to what pulls you in and what makes
you wince. Both are useful information. Send me a handful of either and we'll
plan from there.

## Which session fits

**Short** — a focused set of just you, one look, one setting. Enough for a frame
and an announcement.

**Standard** — you on your own, then with your partner, then with your older
children, with time between so nobody's rushed.

**Extended** — more than one look, more than one setting, and space to move
between outdoors and somewhere at home if that's what you want.
