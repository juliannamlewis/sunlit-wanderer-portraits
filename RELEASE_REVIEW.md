# Quarterly Release & Portfolio Review

A standing checkpoint for keeping the galleries legally clean and gradually
replacing family and friend images with paying clients.

**How this works:** Claude can't contact you or run on a schedule — everything
happens inside a chat you open. So set four recurring calendar reminders that
say *"open the release review with Claude"* and point at this file. Open a chat,
say you're doing the quarterly release review, and we'll work through the list
below together.

**Review dates:** January 15 · April 15 · July 15 · October 15

---

## Each review, walk through these

1. **What came back?** Which signed releases arrived since the last review, and
   which galleries can now use those images?
2. **What's still outstanding?** Which prior clients haven't returned a release
   yet, and is it worth one more ask?
3. **What can be replaced?** Every family or friend image currently standing in
   for a paying client — is there now a released client image that could take
   its place?
4. **What's thin?** Which galleries are still under the 4–10 range for locations
   or under a respectable count for categories?
5. **Anyone aged into adulthood?** Courtesy check, not a legal one — people
   photographed as children who are now adults are worth a text before their
   childhood images stay up another year.
6. **Anything to pull?** Images that no longer represent current work, or where
   a relationship or permission has changed.
