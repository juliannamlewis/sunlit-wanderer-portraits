## Prepare And Logistics

**Q:** What should we wear?

**A:** Start with two to four pieces you already own and feel good in, then build a loose color palette around those. Mix solids with a pattern or two — one or two patterned outfits against complementary solids almost always photographs better than everyone in identical solids.

Try to avoid strongly clashing colors, everyone in competing patterns, big logos (unless they're intentional), and neon shoes if you can help it. Clean, reasonably neutral shoes are perfect, and they're often barely visible anyway.

For fall, think dark green, brown, cream, maroon, burnt orange — those muted seasonal tones. For spring and summer, blues, pinks, yellows work beautifully. Bright and cheerful is fine, just not so loud it pulls the eye off your faces.

Is this for a specific type of session? I can give you a bit more guidance if you let me know whether it's a family session, grad, maternity or couples.
