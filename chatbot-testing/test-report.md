## Prepare And Logistics

**Q:** What should we wear?

**A:** Coordinate without matching. You don't need a new wardrobe.

Start with two to four pieces you already own and like. Build a loose palette around those. Mix solids with a pattern or two — one or two patterned outfits against complementary solids almost always photographs better than everyone in identical solids.

Try to avoid strongly clashing colors, everyone in competing patterns, big logos or text unless they're intentional, and neon shoes if you can help it.

**Fall:** dark green, brown, cream, maroon, burnt orange, muted seasonal tones.

**Spring and summer:** blues, pinks, yellows. Bright and cheerful is fine — just not so loud it pulls the eye off your faces.

**Shoes:** clean, reasonably neutral. Don't overthink them; they're often barely visible.

**Seniors:** clothing and accessories that reflect who they are. A hat, scarf, sunglasses, a jersey.

**Maternity:** solids photograph particularly well.

**Couples:** coordinate rather than match, and avoid two competing patterns.

**Hair and makeup:** look like yourself. Your normal routine is perfect.
